Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information The Success Academy & School System (“the school”, “we”) collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the website or submitting a form, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to send us through the website forms:

  • Admission application: the student’s full name, date of birth, gender, grade applying for, previous school and percentage; the parent or guardian’s name, mobile/WhatsApp number, email address and residential address; an optional CNIC or passport number; and the campus preference.
  • Parent enquiry form: the parent or guardian’s name, phone number, email address (if given), the grade level of interest, and your message.
  • Campus visit request: your name, phone number, preferred date and time slot.
  • Newsletter: your email address.
  • Technical data: like most websites, the servers that deliver this page receive basic technical details such as your IP address and browser type. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers on this website.

Admission forms are completed by parents or guardians. We ask only for the details needed to process an application. The CNIC or passport number is optional.

2. How we use your information

  • To review admission applications and contact you about them.
  • To answer enquiries and arrange campus visits.
  • To send school updates and newsletters to people who subscribed.
  • To keep school records and run the school safely and properly.
  • To meet legal or regulatory requirements when they apply.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Who handles your information

To run the website we use a few service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • FormSubmit delivers the contents of the enquiry, visit and admission forms to the school’s email inbox.
  • Supabase stores newsletter subscriber emails, published school notices and gallery posts.
  • Our email provider holds the form submissions we receive.
  • Content delivery services (such as Google Fonts and public script and icon libraries) load the fonts, icons and code the page needs, so your browser connects to them directly when you visit.

Some of these providers may store or process data in other countries. We share information outside the school only with these providers or when the law requires it.

4. Photos and school activities

The gallery shows photos from school events and activities. If you do not want a photo of your child, or yourself, to appear on the website, contact us and we will remove it promptly.

5. How long we keep information

We keep admission and enquiry information for as long as it is needed for admissions and school records, and newsletter emails until you ask to be removed. When information is no longer needed, we delete it.

6. Keeping information safe

Access to submitted information is limited to authorised school staff, and the website’s management area requires a private login. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Please do not send sensitive details through the website forms beyond what the forms ask for.

7. Your choices

You may contact us at any time to:

  • ask what information we hold about you or your child;
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • ask us to delete your information, or unsubscribe from the newsletter.

We will respond as soon as reasonably possible. Some admission or school records may need to be kept where the law or school rules require it.

8. Links to other websites

This website may link to other sites, such as our social media pages. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.
